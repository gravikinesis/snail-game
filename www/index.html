Background tracking

Background tracking uses your location while the app is closed or not in use, so the snail keeps following where you really are.

While it's on, it is used for: moving the snail toward you, counting distance traveled, picking up salt, and alerting you about close calls, the snail catching up, and gold salt.

Your location stays on your phone. It is never shared or used for ads. A notification shows the whole time it's on, and you can turn it off anytime.

🐌 The Snail

A snail has spawned exactly one day of crawling away from you. It is crawling straight toward you, and it never stops, even when this page is closed.

You can outwalk it easily. The danger is sitting still.

Salt crystals appear at random times. Get to one to freeze the snail where it is. The rarer the salt, the farther the walk.

Gray 1h · 150–700 mBlue 2h · 0.7–1.3 kmGold 4h · 1.3–2 km · rare, gone in 1h

Stay safe: salt is placed at random, so some may land somewhere you can't or shouldn't go. Skip those. Driving away is allowed, but never use the app while you're the one driving.
